BLIP

ABOUT THIS EXPERIMENT

Blip Arcade is an experiment in making software together with machines. Every game, every line of code and every pixel of the interface was made in conversation with Claude (Anthropic), Codex (OpenAI) and GitHub Copilot (GitHub). The human brought the ideas; the machines did most of the typing.

The question behind it: how far can a conversation go before it becomes a piece of software? Quite far, as it turns out.

► A tribute, not a copy

Blip Arcade is a tribute to the golden age of arcade games and the games that followed it: Pong, Breakout, Space Invaders, Asteroids, Snake, 1942, Street Fighter II and many more. The studios and engineers who made the originals shaped the whole history of interactive entertainment.

The games here are new works inspired by those classics. They contain no code, graphics or sound from the originals; everything was written from scratch for this project.

Blip Arcade is strictly non-commercial. It charges no admission, serves no advertising and keeps nothing about you: your best scores stay in your own browser. It exists as a technical and creative experiment, and as a small thank-you to the people who invented the medium.

Every game in this cabinet was made at one table, by people and machines taking turns. This is the crew on the night shift.
