CONTROLS

HOW TO PLAY ON PC AND MOBILE

Play on iPhone and iPad

Tap a game on the Home Screen to play it. A game opened this way is free to play, and its picture is clean and sharp, without the cabinet’s CRT effects.

To go back, tap Home in the top-left corner. To see a game’s controls while you play, tap the question mark at the top of the screen, then tap Guide for the game’s full page.

To keep a game on your own Home Screen, touch and hold its icon, then tap Add to Home Screen and follow the steps. A game added this way opens straight into play.

To arrange the icons, touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen until the icons jiggle, then drag them where you want them. Tap Done when you finish.

Choosing a game

Walk the aisle with the left and right arrow keys, the stick, or the arrows at the foot of the screen. Drop a coin in the door of the machine you want, then press its Start button, Space or Enter.

Whenever you need a hand, lift this manual from its holder on the top bar. The contents lead straight to each game’s chapter. Turn a page with the folded corners, a swipe or the left and right arrow keys, and press Escape to put the book back.

► Gamepad

A connected controller is picked up automatically. The left stick or D-pad moves; the bottom face button (A / ×) is Button 1 and the right face button (B / ◯) is Button 2. Select inserts a coin. The stick and buttons on the screen move with whatever the pad is doing.

► Touch

On a phone or tablet the games start with touch controls: you play on the glass itself. Open the game’s How to play card to switch between Touch, Joystick and D-pad; your choice is remembered. Rally keeps its two dials.

AdderDrag left or right to steer. Tap to start.
BouncerDrag and the paddle follows your finger. Tap to launch the ball.
BrawlerDrag to move. Touch PUNCH or KICK to strike.
BubblerEvery touch blows a bubble. Slide to run, swipe up to jump.
DefenderDrag to move the cannon; it fires while your finger is down.
MeteorsDrag to turn and thrust; hold FIRE to shoot, tap HYPER to escape.
RaiderDrag to steer and throttle. Hold FIRE to shoot.
RallyDrag up or down to move your bat. With two players each has a half.
RoundsDrag to steer and pedal. Touch LEFT or RIGHT to throw a paper.
SerpentSwipe the way you want the snake to turn. Tap to start.
WarlockDrag to move. Touch BOLT to cast, REVEAL to light up the dark.

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger picture and room for both thumbs. On phones that support it, a light buzz answers hits and taps.

► Two players

Six games take two players at one screen: Adder, Brawler, Bubbler, Rally, Rounds and Warlock. A two-player game takes a coin from each player, as the cabinets did. When a second player comes in, the panel grows a second station: player 1 on the left, player 2 on the right.

Player 1WASD, F and G
Player 2The arrow keys, J and K
RallyPress 2 on the title screen, or turn the right-hand dial. Player 2 moves with I/K.

Each game’s chapter tells how a second player joins in.

► The dial — Rally only

Rally puts away the joystick and gives each player a rotary dial instead: player 1 on the left, player 2 on the right. Twist a dial and your bat slides with it; spin it faster and the bat moves faster. On the title screen, tap the left dial for a game against the computer, or the right dial for two players.

Why a dial? The original Pong (Atari, 1972) was played with a knob on the cabinet for each player: turn it and your paddle slides up and down. The dial here is a small tribute to that knob; the story of the machine is in Rally’s chapter.
